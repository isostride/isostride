label: "A/B verdict rule"
action: "give the verdict \"Change\" when the uncertainty interval of the difference excludes zero and the difference exceeds the noise threshold, and \"no detectable change\" otherwise"
ears_type: "ubiquitous"
rationale: "Acceptance: a random split returns \"no detectable change\" in at least 9 of 10 trials. On 26 Nov 2026 a review of the whole app (the runner's \"przegladnij aplikacje raz jeszcze ... pod katem modeli ale nie tylko\"), found the interval a percentile bootstrap over single efforts: too narrow for the few samples a comparison has (at 4 a side it covered zero 85 % of the time for 95 %), and a verdict shown for every metric chosen with no word on how many are judged at once; the runner: \"tak zacznij od poczzatku ... i przejdz przez wszystkie\". Model's choices: the run is the unit, each by the mean of its efforts; the interval is Welch's for two samples of unequal spread (Student's t of the Welch–Satterthwaite degrees of freedom, rounded down) on the runs' means, a trend's that of the slope of the line through the runs' means (Student's t of n − 2); with several metrics judged at once a note says that about one comparison in twenty of the same thing reads a change. On 26 Nov 2026 a third review (the runner: zmergowane, zrob kolejna iteracje) found the interval of a comparison corrected for pace too narrow: the values were moved by a slope estimated from the same runs and then read as plain measurements (with no effect at all, 2 comparisons of 10 of groups two spreads of pace apart read Change). Corrected, the interval is that of the difference at one pace from one fit of the group and the pace, on n − 3 degrees of freedom; raw, Welch as before."
