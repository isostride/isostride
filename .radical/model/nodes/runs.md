App shell: collapsible icon sidebar on the left (Runs, Analysis, A/B comparison, Gear and tags, Settings; theme switch Light/Dark/System and the map background at the bottom), the folder and ⌘K search at its top. The Runs screen: the title with a switch "Calendar / List" and the actions Recompute everything and Import folder; the import's progress and messages; the weeks at a glance: this week's distance (big), its time and runs and its change against the 4-week average, the 4-week average per week, all the runs and since when, and on the right the distance of the last 12 weeks as bars, the current week in the accent, a week's figures on hover. Calendar (the default), the whole width of the window: the weeks grouped by month under a band with the month's name and totals (distance, time, runs) that stays at the top while its weeks scroll (a week across two months goes with its Thursday's, the other month's days shaded); a row per week, newest first, named by its ISO number and dates ("W45 · 2–8 Nov"), the days Monday to Sunday (today circled), each run a tile with its distance, time and pace and its title or type, opening the run; at the end of the row the week's distance, time, runs, average pace and heart rate, or "rest". List: the table of runs (date, start, title, distance, time, pace, average heart rate, heart rate source, shoes, tags, load 7 d) with Edit opening the tag editor (title, shoe override, type, technique cue, note).
