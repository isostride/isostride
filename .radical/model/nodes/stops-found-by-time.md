rationale: "The runner asked whether the app works with other watches (\"czy aplikacja zadziala z inymi zegarkami ? garmin itp ?\"): with Garmin's Smart recording a window held fewer than 5 samples and counted as stopped; the fix in the same change (\"W tym samym PR\"). Model's choices: a sample covers at most 10 s, so a pause of the watch (no samples) stays a stop; 0.5 m/s as before; 1 to 7 s is the model's reading of how often Smart recording records. On 26 Nov 2026 a review of the whole app (the runner's \"przegladnij aplikacje raz jeszcze ... pod katem modeli ale nie tylko\"), found the segments taking every gap of more than 2 s between samples for a pause, while the windows take a sample to cover up to 10 s: a run recorded smart (a sample every 1 to 7 s) had no moving sample in any segment, and every effort of it was left out; the runner: \"tak zacznij od poczzatku ... i przejdz przez wszystkie\". Model's choices: for the segments a gap is a pause past 2 s in a run recorded every second and past 10 s in one recorded smart, told by the median gap of the run; in a run recorded smart each sample counts by the time to the next one (10 s at most) in the means and in the seconds stopped."
