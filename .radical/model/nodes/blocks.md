Two sections of cards. "Suggested" at the top: recommendation cards (e.g. "Seaside promenade · 1.1 km · 34 passes", "25-minute tempo · 6 workouts") with a mini map or lap sparkline and "Accept" / "Dismiss" buttons; dismissed ones disappear for good. "My blocks" below: one card per definition with type icon, origin badge (manual / recommendation), instance count with accepted/rejected split, last instance date and quick links "Trend" and "Compare A/B". Clicking a card opens a right-side sheet with the definition's parameters and editable tolerances (pace band, grade, route match, steadiness, effort before, HR start), showing defaults and a "Reset to default" per field; saving triggers re-detection with a progress chip.
