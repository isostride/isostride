rationale: "The runner asked: \"w capacity best efforts mam osobny graf na pace i power ... czy to nie to samo .. ?\"; the model answered that they are not the same (uphill, into the wind or on soft ground the power is high and the pace slow) and offered one table, a row per length with both bests, marking where they come from different runs; the runner said \"tak\", then, of it pinned as a third of the Dashboard: \"popraw tabele ... zobacz jak wyglada na 1/3 ekranu\". Model's choices: one effort when the two bests are of the same run and their stretches overlap; without power the table is of pace alone; by the card's width the heart rate's column goes first (below 680 px), then the effort's, shown as a dot by the run (below 480 px), the units moving to the heads and a run's title giving way; days without the year when this year's."
