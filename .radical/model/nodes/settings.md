The Settings screen (/settings), in the navigation. Section "Zones": one row per metric (pace, heart rate, cadence, power and the others): its name and unit, a field of the boundaries between zones separated by commas (pace as m:ss), and the zones they give as chips Z1 … Zn with their ranges, in the metric's colour from light to deep; for heart rate a field "Max HR" and a button "Fill from max HR" (60, 70, 80, 90 %). Save writes user/settings.json; a note says the zones colour the run chart.
