The picker of the Analysis screen, opened by "+ Add block or run", with two tabs. "Blocks and runs": a search box over one list in three groups, Your blocks (kept ones, with their kind), Suggested, found in your runs (e.g. "1.0 km northeast-bound · route passed 43 times in 43 runs; 17 meet the conditions", each with Dismiss; taking one keeps it and adds it), and Runs (date, distance, title); the arrow keys move, Enter adds. "New block": the builder, Segment (Whole runs | Laps | Fastest stretch | Steady stretch) with its length (5 min … 10 km or a number and unit; "kept for at least" for a steady stretch), In which runs (period presets and dates, workout type, shoes, title contains; more: technique cue, heart rate from, watch, laps), The segment on average (pace, heart rate, and for runs, laps and steady stretches distance and duration, each from–to), The segment at every moment (heart rate never below / above, pace always within), More (start, the fastest or every one from each run, statistic), the name written from the rule, and the live count "15 segments in 15 runs" with the newest listed; then "Use in analysis" (the rule lives in the link) or "Save and use" (kept). From a block's chip on the Analysis screen, Edit opens the block sheet: the same builder for a rule, or the name and matching tolerances (pace band, grade, steadiness, effort before, heart rate from minute, route match, start) for a block found in the runs, with Save and re-detect and Delete block.
