label: "Estimated pace curve"
action: "draw beside each measured pace curve an estimated one, fitted to the item's bests as an envelope with every parameter from the fit (a best above the curve costing many times more than one below it, an easy run far below not pulling it), so that no measured best lies above it: within 2 to 20 minutes the critical speed model's hyperbola, past 20 minutes Riegel's power law fitted to the bests of 3 minutes and more, dotted as an extrapolation, with the predicted times for 5 km, 10 km, the half and the marathon; show each best's gap to the estimate as a stroke between them, and read the estimate at any length of time under the pointer, with the nearest best and its gap; under 2 minutes measure no gap and shade none, keeping the bests with a note that no model describes that length; give the critical speed and D' from the critical model; with fewer than 3 such bests, say the estimate needs them"
ears_type: "ubiquitous"
rationale: "The runner asked: \"jesli chodzi o pace curve ... dodaj estymaowana ... bazujac na researchu\", and, seeing bests above a regression line (\"mam wyniki ponad ta linia\"), chose the envelope: \"tak zrob (1, obwiednia)\"; then \"daj mozliwosc przemieszczania sie po tej estymacie ... albo pokaz roznice pomiedzy pomiarem a estymata\", both done. Later: \"czy krzywa tempa i mocy jest napewno prostolinijna ?\", \"ok\" to the hyperbola within 2–20 minutes and the power law dotted past them; \"Poniżej 2 min w modelu Riegla nie liczyć różnicy i nie rysować wypełnienia. Punkty zostają, ale z dopiskiem, że żaden model tej długości nie opisuje\"; \"żaden najlepszy wynik nie może leżeć nad nią ... punkt nad krzywą kosztuje w dopasowaniu wielokrotnie więcej niż punkt pod nią\"; \"myslalem ze dopasowanie krzywej polega na tym ze dopasowujesz sie do pomiarow i liczysz przez to parametry modelu\", then \"idz ta metoda\". The models are the literature's (ADR \"Pace curve models\"). The model's choices: the weight 1000, the reach of 3 %, the power law from bests of 3 minutes and more, the critical model from 2 to 20 minutes, 3 bests at least for either, the extrapolation to an hour, and the distances predicted, 5 km, 10 km, the half and the marathon."
