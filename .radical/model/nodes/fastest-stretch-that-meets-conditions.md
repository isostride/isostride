The model's choices: the conditions are applied while searching, so the answer is the fastest stretch that qualifies and not the fastest stretch checked afterwards; a stretch may start at any second of the run; at most 30 stretches come from one run. On 26 Nov 2026 a review of the whole app (the runner's \"przegladnij aplikacje raz jeszcze ... pod katem modeli ale nie tylko\"), found every stretch of a length 2 s short (the fastest 5 minutes lasted 298 s: the 2 s meant for a stretch ending on the last sample of a run were given to every start), and the distance of a segment one sample short of its duration (1 km in 240 s read 996 m, a pace of 4:01 for 4:00); the runner: \"tak zacznij od poczzatku ... i przejdz przez wszystkie\". Model's choices: a stretch lasts its whole length, only one ending on the last sample of the run may fall 2 s short; a segment's distance runs to its end, between the last sample inside it and the first after it by the time, not across a pause; the algorithms' version is 9, so the segments are found again."
