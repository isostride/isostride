The Performance screen (/performance), full width, in the navigation between Analysis and Settings. Kept simple: a model shows its name, its value, its chart and its trust label; everything else is behind its "Why?". Tabs: Overview | Load | Capacity | Aerobic | Response | Model, the tab kept in the link (tab=…); a group's tab has a second switch of its views (view=…). Overview: a card per group (Load: what you did, from every run; Capacity: what you can do, from your all-out efforts; Aerobic: how efficiently you run, from everyday runs and their heart rate; Response: how load turns into performance), each a list with one line per model: its name (a link to its view; a model computed from another indented under it, with an arrow), what it says now, a trust chip (a dot and Reliable, Rough, Not reliable or No data) and "Why?". "Why?" opens a panel under the line, one at a time: the trust and its reason in a sentence; on the left "Assumptions", each a line with a status dot, the assumption, "holds", "in doubt", "does not hold" or "you must judge", and what the data show; on the right "Rests on" (chips: the models with their trust dot as links, the data and settings), "What it is" (what it tells and its method) and "How to read it". A group's view: its models in one line (name, trust chip, Why?), the opened Why panel as a card under the line, then the figures (a number and its name; a short comparison with 90 days before where there is one) and the charts with a short title each, no notes under them. Capacity opens on Best efforts: a table for pace and one for power, a row per length of 2–20 min with the best, the effort (all-out, hard, not all-out), the share of the maximum heart rate, and the run as a link that opens it with the stretch selected. Model: the load's trust and Why? above the load model's settings.
