The Ask screen (/ask): a card of controls in rows, Answer (Change over time | Fastest stretch | Highest or lowest), Metric (pills; with Highest | Lowest for a ranking) or Stretch (pills 5 min … 10 km and a number with its unit), Period (All runs | Last 30 days | Last 3 months | This year, and two dates), Filters (workout type, shoes, title contains, top N). The answer recomputes as a control changes: a progress bar while the worker reads the runs, then the answer card with the question in words, the sentence (in the accent when it is a change), the Over time chart (each run's value, the fitted line and noise band for a change) and the table of runs behind the answer (rank, date, title, value, the stretch for a fastest question, Open run → with the stretch selected). The question lives in the link.
