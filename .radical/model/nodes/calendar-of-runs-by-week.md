rationale: "The runner said: \"dopracuj widok biegow ... wyglada kiepsko\", then asked: \"moze kalendarz ?\", \"statystyki na tydzien ?\" and \"miesiac pokazuj lepiej na kalnaderzu ... trzeba chyba pogrupowac wiersze\". The model's choices: a row per week, newest first, Monday to Sunday (weeks begin on Monday), empty weeks kept so gaps show; the weeks grouped by month under a band with the month's name and totals (distance, time, runs of the runs dated in it) that stays at the top while its weeks scroll; a week across two months belongs to the month of its Thursday, as ISO weeks are counted, the other month's days dimmed; the left column names the week by its ISO number and dates; a run is a tile with its distance, time, pace and type, and opens on a click; the week's totals are its distance, moving time, number of runs and average pace (distance over time); above, this week against the average of the 4 weeks before, and the distance of the last 12 weeks as bars with the current week marked; a switch \"Calendar / List\" keeps the table of runs and its tag editor, the choice remembered in the browser; days are the runner's local days."
