The load of a run and the form over time: a summary of each run's effort kept in derived/load-summaries.json (seconds at each heart rate, the normalised effort speed and power, the bests of 2–20 minutes), the four load models (Edwards and Banister TRIMP, rTSS, Power TSS) with their thresholds from the critical speed and power of the 90 days up to each run, and the Banister fitness–fatigue model with the acute:chronic ratio.
