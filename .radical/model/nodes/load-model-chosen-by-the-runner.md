action: "compute the load of every run with that model — Edwards TRIMP (minutes in the heart rate zones set in Settings times the zone's number, none under 50 % of the maximum heart rate), Banister TRIMP (minutes weighted by the heart rate reserve, from the resting and maximum heart rate and the sex), rTSS (the normalised effort pace against the threshold pace) or Power TSS (the normalised power against the critical power) — the threshold pace and critical power taken from the curve of the 90 days up to each run when its short bests show all-out efforts, else the last threshold so taken, unless set by hand, and show the run's load on the Run screen and each week's load in the calendar"
ears_type: "event-driven"
rationale: "The runner asked: \"chcialbym miec model ktory modeluje performance .... np. TRIMP, critical pawer based ... chce aby model byl ustawiany przez uzytkownika w ustawieniach\", and chose the load models \"TRIMP Edwardsa, TRIMP Banistera, rTSS (tempo), Power TSS (CP)\"; later: \"mam wrazenie ze wszystkie modele sa pomieszane... myslalem ze w zaleznosci od wybranego modelu banister etc ... inne metryki beda dostepne a inne nie\", \"tak\" to each choice saying what it needs, then \"ok wybor modelu chce w settings\". On 25 Nov 2026, asked to check the models (\"upewnij sie ze sa popranie zimplementowane\"), the review found the automatic threshold falling to the easy pace when the 90 days hold no hard effort; the runner: \"ok popraw wszystko pokolei\"; of the points left, Edwards' TRIMP counting the time under 50 % of the maximum as 1: \"zrob je\". Model's choices: Banister TRIMP as the default; Edwards' bands from the zones of Settings, the time under 50 % of the maximum heart rate weighing nothing, as in Edwards 1993; the maximum heart rate taken from the runs (the highest mean of a 10 s window, a run's peak more than 5 bpm above every other run's left out as a sensor's spike) unless set, the resting heart rate 60 until set; normalisation as a 30 s rolling mean raised to the 4th power; the threshold pace equal to the critical speed and the threshold power to the critical power of the bests of 2–20 min in the 90 days up to the run, taken only when D′ (W′) is worth 12 s or more at that critical speed (power) — less means the short bests were held back — else the last threshold so taken holds, and the runs before the first take the first; a run without the data its model needs gets its duration times the runner's median load per minute, marked estimated; the run's load on the Run screen and the week's load in the calendar; each choice saying what it needs and how much of it the runs have, one the runs give nothing for not to be chosen; the choice and the values saved together, the loads recomputed; on Load and Response a line naming the model in use, leading to Settings. On 26 Nov 2026 a review of the whole app (the runner's \"przegladnij aplikacje raz jeszcze ... pod katem modeli ale nie tylko\"), found a value outside its range replaced by its default in silence while the form read \"Saved\", values that contradict each other accepted (fatigue as long as fitness, a maximum heart rate under the resting one), and \"Save and recompute\" writing back the thresholds of the checks as they were when the card opened; the runner: \"tak zacznij od poczzatku ... i przejdz przez wszystkie\". Model's choices: such values are marked and named with their ranges and not saved; the thresholds of the checks are kept as last saved; a file holding values that contradict each other reads the maximum as left to the app and the time constants as the defaults."
